# ScaleNova Social Media Management Framework

## 1. Content Calendar & Design Process

1. **Monthly Content Planning**: Defining 8 to 16 educational, promotional, and industry posts per month.
2. **Graphic Design & Copywriting**: Crafting branded visual assets, carousels, and engaging captions tailored for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.
3. **Review & Scheduling**: Shared approval preview link for client feedback prior to publishing.

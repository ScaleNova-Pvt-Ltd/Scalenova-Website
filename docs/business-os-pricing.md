# ScaleNova Business OS — Pricing Architecture

## 1. Plan Structure & Pricing Tiers

| Plan Name | Monthly Price (+ GST) | Annual Price (+ GST) | Target Audience | User Capacity | Storage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core OS** | **Rs 4,899 / mo** | Rs 48,990 / yr (2 mos free) | Startups & Small Businesses | Up to 3 Users | 1 GB Cloud Storage |
| **Growth OS** | **Rs 13,699 / mo** | Rs 1,36,990 / yr (2 mos free) | Growing MSMEs, Agencies & Teams | Up to 25 Users | 2 GB Cloud Storage |
| **Elite OS** | **Rs 35,799 / mo** | Rs 3,57,990 / yr (2 mos free) | Established MSMEs & Enterprises | Up to 100 Users | 4 GB+ Cloud Storage |

---

## 2. Non-Duplication Display Principle

Inherited plan capabilities are strictly referenced as Includes Core OS or Includes Growth OS to keep pricing cards clean, readable, and mobile-friendly:

- **Core OS**: Premium Website + Lead Capture, CRM + Core Business Management, Basic SEO + Online Presence, Training, Support + Monthly Reports.
- **Growth OS**: Includes Core OS + Sales & Accounts, HR & Payroll, Inventory & Projects, SEO & Social Media.
- **Elite OS**: Includes Growth OS + Complete ERP, Manufacturing & Assets, Advanced Digital Marketing, Business Intelligence & Reports, Dedicated Account Support.

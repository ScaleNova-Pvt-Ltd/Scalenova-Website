# ScaleNova Website — Lead Capture Architecture

## 1. Primary Conversion Journey

The website centers on a single high-conversion action: **Book a Live Demo**.

---

## 2. Lead Form Schema & Fields

- **Full Name**: Customer contact name.
- **Business Name**: Firm / Company name.
- **Work Email**: Official business email.
- **Phone / WhatsApp**: Active mobile contact number for India.
- **Primary Requirement**: Selectable dropdown (All-in-One Business OS, CRM & Sales, ERP & Stock, HR & Attendance, Executive BI).
- **Message / Notes**: Optional specific business workflow details.
- **UTM Metadata**: Automatically appended parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, referral_source, landing_page).

# ScaleNova Business OS — Platform Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary

ScaleNova Business OS is a managed business operating system designed for Indian MSMEs. It integrates digital presence, lead capture, CRM, ERP, HR, financial management, and business intelligence into one unified ecosystem.

---

## 2. Integrated Product Layers

Website / Lead Capture -> CRM & Sales -> ERP & Operations -> BI & Reports -> Ongoing Support

1. **Digital Presence & Lead Capture**: Premium business website, automated lead capture, UTM parameter tracking.
2. **CRM & Sales Operations**: Kanban lead pipelines, customer interaction histories, quotations, and automated follow-ups.
3. **ERP & Business Management**: Inventory, multi-godown stock management, purchase orders, GST billing, HR, payroll, and project tracking.
4. **Digital Growth Services**: Search Engine Optimization (SEO), social media management, content design, and local business directory listings.
5. **Business Intelligence & Analytics**: Real-time operational dashboards, financial summaries, and executive performance reports.
6. **Managed Customer Success**: Hands-on team onboarding, dedicated support contacts, regular operational reviews, and system maintenance.

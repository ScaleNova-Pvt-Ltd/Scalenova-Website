# ScaleNova Website — Content Architecture & Narrative Flow

## 1. Landing Page Narrative Flow

1. **Hero**: Headline (*One System Every Business Needs*), value promise, 3D lattice visualizer, primary CTA (*Book a Live Demo*).
2. **MSME Pain Points**: Real operational friction (scattered leads, missed follow-ups, spreadsheet chaos).
3. **ScaleNova Answer (Before vs. After)**: Transitioning from fragmented tools to one managed Business OS.
4. **Visual Workflows**: Operations, Digital Growth, and Customer Success flow diagrams.
5. **Business OS Feature Ecosystem**: 5 visual categories (Build, Manage, Grow, Understand, Stay Supported).
6. **Client Delivery Model**: 8-step onboarding and management journey.
7. **Platform Technology**: Honest positioning of underlying Frappe and ERPNext technology.
8. **Pricing Architecture**: Core OS (Rs 4,899), Growth OS (Rs 13,699), Elite OS (Rs 35,799) with Monthly/Annual toggle.
9. **Careers & Partner Network**: Overview cards linking to full SPA drawer views.
10. **FAQ & Conversion CTA**: Direct connection options and lead capture form.

# ScaleNova Digital Marketing & Online Growth Services

## 1. Managed Service Scope

ScaleNova provides ongoing digital growth services alongside the Business OS software platform:

1. **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**: Technical health checks, on-page content optimization, metadata updates, and local search visibility.
2. **Social Media Management**: Content calendar planning, custom post graphics design, caption writing, post scheduling, and performance reviews.
3. **Digital Business Presence**: Google Business Profile optimization, business directory consistency management, and brand alignment.

---

## 2. Commercial Approval Model

Clients may choose to review and self-publish content calendars or authorize ScaleNova to publish scheduled posts on their behalf.

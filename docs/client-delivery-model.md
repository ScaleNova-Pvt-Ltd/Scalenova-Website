# ScaleNova Client Delivery & Onboarding Model

## 1. The 8-Step Customer Journey

Understand -> Configure -> Build -> Connect -> Train -> Support -> Grow -> Review

1. **Understand**: Discovery of client business workflows, sales stages, and inventory structures.
2. **Configure**: Provisioning and configuring the ScaleNova Business OS cloud instance.
3. **Build**: Designing the client's premium web presence and lead capture experience.
4. **Connect**: Linking website lead forms directly to the CRM lead pipeline.
5. **Train**: Live role-based walkthroughs for sales, accounts, operations, and HR teams.
6. **Support**: Dedicated technical support contact for operational assistance.
7. **Grow**: Execution of SEO, social media management, and online presence activities.
8. **Review**: Monthly operational reporting, performance reviews, and system enhancements.

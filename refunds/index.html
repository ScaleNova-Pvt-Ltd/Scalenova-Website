Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • ScaleNova Private Limited

1. Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your monthly subscription at any time by giving written notice via email to support@scalenovasys.com or contacting your dedicated account manager at least 7 days prior to your next renewal date.

2. Monthly Subscriptions

Because ScaleNova allocates dedicated cloud infrastructure, configuration labor, and ongoing support time upon subscription initiation, fees paid for the active monthly cycle are non-refundable. Your service remains fully operational until the end of the current paid billing cycle.

3. Annual Subscriptions

If you cancel an annual subscription mid-term, unused full calendar months will be recalculated at standard monthly non-discounted rates, and the remaining pro-rata balance will be refunded within 14 business days.

4. Complete Data Export on Exit

Upon cancellation, your data remains accessible for 30 calendar days during which you can download a full, unencrypted SQL database backup and file archive at zero extra charge.

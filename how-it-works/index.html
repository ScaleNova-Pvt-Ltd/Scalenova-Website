The 7-Step Operating Lifecycle

How ScaleNova Delivers Calm & Structure

We do not hand you a complex software login and leave you to figure it out. ScaleNova guides your business through a disciplined, hands-on 7-stage operating lifecycle.

Schedule a Discovery Call Compare Operating Plans
01

Understand

Phase 1

We meet with business leadership to conduct an operational intake: analyzing your current bottlenecks, cataloging existing spreadsheets, examining lead sources (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, WhatsApp, referrals), and identifying where operational handoffs are currently failing.

Outcome: Verified Operational Blueprint & System Configuration Roadmap
02

Set Up

Phase 2

ScaleNova provisions your secure private cloud workspace, sets up SSL certificates, configures your company domain DNS, and establishes your 8-folder Client Master Archive. We onboard current active operational data (customers, vendors, items, opening balances).

Outcome: Secure Cloud Instance & Clean Master Data Registers
03

Connect

Phase 3

We interconnect your front-facing digital touchpoints with back-office operations: website inquiry forms route directly into CRM pipeline stages, quotation templates link to billing registers, and inventory records tie into purchasing workflows.

Outcome: Seamless Interconnected End-to-End Business Flow
04

Train

Phase 4

Software fails when employees don't know how to use it. ScaleNova conducts live 90-minute orientation workshops tailored by role (Core: 1 session, Growth: 3 sessions, Elite: 5 workshops). We teach sales how to log leads and quotes, and accounts how to record receipts.

Outcome: Confident, Trained Team Members and Standardized SOPs
05

Manage

Phase 5

This is what separates ScaleNova from an agency or software vendor. We stay actively involved: updating web copy, fixing broken links, maintaining local directory listings, creating monthly social graphics, maintaining backups, and answering staff workflow questions.

Outcome: Ongoing Operational Peace of Mind without Hiring IT Staff
06

Review

Phase 6

We meet with the business owner on a disciplined schedule (Monthly for Core OS, Biweekly for Growth OS, Weekly for Elite OS). We review lead volume, conversion bottlenecks, invoice aging, and stock status so you stay in total control without getting lost in weeds.

Outcome: Disciplined Accountability & Executive Operational Visibility
07

Improve

Phase 7

As your sales team grows or new product branches open, ScaleNova adjusts your operating system: expanding pipeline stages, adding automated reorder triggers, refining quotation stationery, and introducing custom executive dashboards.

Outcome: A Business Operating System That Scales With Your Enterprise

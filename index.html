General Enquiries & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers regarding implementation, data safety, and system capabilities.

What is ScaleNova Business OS and how does it help Indian MSMEs?

ScaleNova Business OS is a single, centralized operating platform built specifically for Indian MSMEs (clinics, real estate, agencies, consultants). It replaces fragmented third-party tools by combining CRM lead management, ERP inventory, employee attendance, and invoicing into one unified cloud dashboard.

How fast can our business be onboarded and operational?

Most MSME setups are completely configured, tested, and operational within 3 to 5 business days. Our engineering team handles all the initial workflow setups, data migration, and staff training sessions.

Can we upgrade or adjust our OS plan as our team expands?

Yes. You can start on the Core OS plan and seamlessly upgrade to Growth OS or Elite OS as your branch locations, headcount, or customer volume increases, without any system downtime.

How does ScaleNova protect my business data?

ScaleNova uses secure cloud hosting, controlled access, and standard security practices to help protect your business information. Access to your data is managed according to your account permissions, while regular platform and infrastructure maintenance helps keep your business environment reliable and protected.

What technical support and training are included with each plan?

Every plan includes a live onboarding session, direct email support (info@scalenovasystems.com), and routine cloud system updates. Elite OS subscribers additionally receive a dedicated priority technical account manager.

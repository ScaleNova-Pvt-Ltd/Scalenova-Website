The 6 Managed Pillars

What ScaleNova Actually Manages For Your Business

ScaleNova is your managed operating partner. We configure, connect, maintain, and review the six critical operational pillars that run the work behind your business.

PILLAR 01

Digital Presence & Web Infrastructure

Hosted & Maintained

Your website should be an active business asset, not an abandoned digital brochure built by an unreachable freelancer. ScaleNova designs, provisions, cloud-hosts, and continuously updates your responsive business website with strict mobile performance and verified SSL security.

Domain & DNS Management

DNS record setup, SPF/DKIM business email records, and uptime monitoring.

Responsive Web Architecture

Fast-loading design adapted for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

Continuous Maintenance

Regular copy edits, product photo updates, security patches, and cloud upkeep.

PILLAR 02

Leads, Inquiries & Customer CRM

Zero Inquiry Leakage

Most MSMEs lose up to 40% of their prospective buyers because inquiries sit scattered across personal WhatsApp chats, Excel sheets, and email threads. ScaleNova routes all web forms, WhatsApp leads, and directory inquiries directly into organized customer records with visual pipeline tracking.

Connected Lead Capture

Structured inquiry forms instantly create CRM prospect profiles with contact details.

Customer History & Deals

Complete chronological timeline of notes, meetings, quotation requests, and deals.

Automated Routing & Alerts

Instant notifications sent to your sales representatives when a new lead arrives.

PILLAR 03

Business Operations, Billing & Workflows

Operational Backbone

Eliminate disconnected desktop tally registers and messy paperwork. ScaleNova sets up interconnected quotation, sales order, invoicing, inventory registers, vendor purchasing, and employee HRMS workflows inside your private cloud environment.

Sales & Invoicing

Turn quotations into confirmed sales invoices with payment recording and balance aging.

Inventory & Stock Registers

Track stock movements, reorder thresholds, warehouse locations, and vendor POs.

People Operations & HRMS

Staff directories, daily check-in logs, leave approvals, and monthly salary slips.

PILLAR 04

Digital Growth, Directories & Visibility

Disciplined Local SEO

We do not sell false "guaranteed #1 ranking" marketing hype. Instead, ScaleNova executes consistent, disciplined local search presence, Google Business profile upkeep, search engine indexing, and business directory management so verified local buyers find your legitimate firm.

Google Business Profile Upkeep

Map pin accuracy, operational hours, photos, and customer review links.

Managed Directory Listings

Active listing management across relevant regional trade portals (up to 4 in Growth, 10 in Elite).

Search Engine Indexing

XML sitemap maintenance, meta tags, and structured data submission to Google Search Console.

PILLAR 05

Content & Business Documentation

Professional Brand Standards

When sending proposals to enterprise clients, presentation matters. ScaleNova produces clean branded document stationery, proposal templates, and monthly branded social media graphics (4/mo in Growth OS, 8/mo in Elite OS) that position your enterprise with credibility.

Branded Document Stationery

Quotation covers, invoice letterheads, and executive pitch templates.

Recurring Social Media Graphics

4 to 8 tailored monthly graphics published across LinkedIn, Instagram, or Facebook.

Client Master Archive

Centralized brand asset library, original logo vectors, and document templates.

PILLAR 06

Support, Team Training & Business Reviews

24-Hr Contact SLA

A system is only as good as the team using it. ScaleNova does not just configure software and walk away. We conduct hands-on live team training, provide continuous helpdesk assistance, and meet with business owners for structured operational reviews.

Live Team Training

Structured 90-min live orientation workshops for sales, billing, and management staff.

Scheduled Operational Reviews

Monthly (Core), Biweekly (Growth), or Weekly (Elite) meetings to analyze system health.

Responsive Helpdesk

Initial contact within 24 business hours via WhatsApp and dedicated support desk.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • ScaleNova Private Limited

1. Executive Commitment to Data Ownership

At ScaleNova Private Limited ("ScaleNova", "we", "our", or "us"), we operate on a fundamental principle: You own 100% of your business data. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or use your commercial data for advertising.

2. Dedicated Tenant & Database Isolation

Every ScaleNova Business OS client receives a dedicated cloud container and database instance. Your sales records, GST invoices, inventory counts, employee information, and customer leads are logically and cryptographically isolated from all other clients.

3. Information Collected & Purpose

  • Account & Onboarding Data: Business name, contact person, billing address, GSTIN, and authorized user credentials.
  • Website Inquiries: Leads submitted via client website contact forms are directly synchronized into the client's own CRM workspace.
  • Telemetry & Operational Health: Server uptime, resource utilization, error logs, and backup integrity metrics to ensure system reliability.

4. Backup & Disaster Recovery

We configure automated daily backups of your complete database and application state with secure offsite replication. In the event of an outage or error, restoration can be executed within our standard SLA window.

5. Data Portability & Exit Rights

If you choose to cancel your ScaleNova subscription, you can request a complete SQL database dump and file archive within 30 calendar days. We will provide clean, unencrypted standard database and file backups with zero proprietary lock-in.

6. Contact Data Privacy Officer

For inquiries regarding data protection, access requests, or security disclosures, please email us at privacy@scalenovasys.com.
